Your report

Understanding your report

Your report opens with a 0–100 health score, then a prioritized list of findings, each with the evidence that triggered it and a staging-first fix. Here is how to read it.

The health score

The score is deterministic — the same snapshot always produces the same number. It starts at 100 and deducts points for each finding by severity:

SeverityPoints deducted
Critical15
High8
Medium3
Low1
Info0

Category caps

Deductions are capped per category, so one noisy area can never zero the score on its own. The score also has a floor of 5 — a live database is never a 0.

CategoryMaximum deduction
Queries30
Indexes25
Ops (locks, connections, growth)25
Config20
Maintenance15
Other10

The score breakdown on the report shows the post-cap deduction per category — e.g. queries −18, indexes −10 — so you can see exactly where the points went.

How findings are prioritized

Findings are ordered by severity first, then by estimated impact, then by stable tie-breakers. The report highlights the top findings so you know what to tackle first. Every finding includes:

  • The evidence — the specific metric and value that triggered the rule (e.g. "this query is 34% of total database time").
  • Why it matters — the performance consequence in plain language.
  • A suggested action — a concrete, staging-first fix.

See the Findings reference for the full rule catalog.

Numbers you can trust When AI narration is enabled, it may only rephrase evidence the rule engine already produced. A digit guard rejects any narration that introduces a number not present in the evidence, so the prose can never invent a metric.

The human review hold

No report is delivered automatically. After the engine runs, your audit is held in expert review — a human checks it before it is released to you. On your dashboard you'll see the status move through:

StatusMeaning
queuedSnapshot received, waiting to be analyzed.
analyzing…The rule engine is running.
expert review in progressHeld for human review — usually released within one business day.
readyApproved and available to download.
failedSomething went wrong; the error is shown on the row. See the FAQ.

What you download

  • Report (PDF or HTML) — the full audit: score, findings, evidence, and fixes.
  • tasks.md — the same findings as paste-ready tickets for GitHub, Jira, or your tracker.

If PDF rendering isn't available in your deployment, the dashboard offers the identical HTML report instead — the content is the same.
